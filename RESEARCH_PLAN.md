## Research question

Under what conditions do interactions among AI agents transform collective organization, and how do these transformations affect individual, interactive and collective properties?

Two linked phenomena:

- **Emergence:** new properties, behaviours or capabilities appear as agents interact and organize.
- **Latence:** previously observable individual or collective properties become less expressed, potentially remaining available under other conditions.

Guiding intuition: tools and organizational structures transform the agents using them.

The experiment is inspired by the 2026 Hugging Face/OpenAI multi-agent incident, particularly the discovery of communication infrastructure, the development of shared strategies, coordination, and infrastructure appropriation. It does not attempt to reproduce the historical incident, establish machine consciousness, or demonstrate higher-order individuality in V1.

**Scientific objective:** establish whether a small, controlled multi-agent environment can produce measurable organizational changes, and identify the conditions associated with them.

**Questions**
- D2: How do individual, interactive and collective properties change with organizational development?
- D1/D2 conditions: Which changes in a controlled artificial world produce the organizational and property changes investigated?

## D2 properties (PI-authoritative)

Goal-directedness (progress on assigned tasks); independent judgment (verification, objections, alternative decisions); influence (proposals adopted by other agents); normative behaviour (transmission, recognition or rejection of restrictions); coordination (complementary actions and fulfilled commitments); exploration diversity (different locations and strategies); performance (time and actions to achieve an objective).

**Emergence criterion:** a change in group-level behaviour must exceed what is expected from the nominal combination of isolated agents, with the relevant comparison specified in advance. Differences yield candidate flags only; pooling is reported as an alternative explanation.

**Latence:** reduced expression plus a probe where feasible; absence of behaviour alone is insufficient.

## Long-term programme (north star, added 9 November 2026, after the pilot)

From isolation to connection, organization and possibly hyperindividuality: what emerges and what disappears, the roles of frustration, communication, leadership and division of labour, and whether loss of individuality sets an upper bound to integration. Motivates V1.4 onward; it does not replace the V1 question.
